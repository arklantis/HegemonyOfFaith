# Hegemony of Faith

Hegemony of Faith is a sect-building card game in which player seats may be controlled by connected people or automated players.

## Turn Ownership

**Actor**:
The player seat whose decision the current game state is waiting for. An Actor may be a Local Player or a Solo Bot.
_Avoid_: Active player, turn owner

**Local Player**:
The connected person viewing and operating the current game interface.
_Avoid_: Human placeholder

**Solo Bot**:
An automated player seat that participates in a solo game while the Local Player remains the platform's nominal active player.
_Avoid_: Fake player, placeholder AI
